MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date of signing 
between the parties ("Disclosing Party" and "Receiving Party").

SECTION 1: CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

1.1 The Receiving Party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary information, 
trade secrets, business plans, and technical data disclosed by the Disclosing Party. This 
information, collectively referred to as "Confidential Information," shall not be disclosed 
to any third party without prior written consent from the Disclosing Party.

1.2 Confidential Information shall be protected using the same degree of care that a 
reasonably prudent entity uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no case 
less than reasonable care. The Receiving Party must implement reasonable safeguards including:
- Limiting access to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis
- Maintaining secure physical and digital storage
- Implementing appropriate security protocols

1.3 The obligations under this section shall apply for a period of 5 (five) years from the 
date of disclosure or for so long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

SECTION 2: TERMINATION OF AGREEMENT

2.1 Termination Conditions
This Agreement may be terminated by either party upon written notice to the other party. The 
termination shall be effective thirty (30) days from the date of written notice ("Termination 
Notice").

2.2 Notice Requirements
The Termination Notice must be delivered via email to the primary contact person of the other 
party or via registered mail to their registered office address. A copy should also be sent to 
their legal department if applicable.

2.3 Post-Termination Obligations
Upon termination, the Receiving Party shall:
- Cease all use of the Confidential Information
- Return or destroy all copies of Confidential Information within 15 days
- Certify in writing that all Confidential Information has been properly disposed of
- Maintain confidentiality obligations for additional two (2) years post-termination

2.4 Survival of Obligations
The confidentiality obligations shall survive the termination of this Agreement for the period 
specified in Section 1.3.

SECTION 3: NON-COMPETE CLAUSE

3.1 Scope of Restriction
During the term of this Agreement and for a period of TWO (2) YEARS following termination, 
the Receiving Party agrees not to:
- Engage in any business that directly competes with the Disclosing Party
- Solicit clients or customers of the Disclosing Party
- Use Confidential Information to develop competing products or services
- Hire employees of the Disclosing Party involved in related projects

3.2 Geographic Limitation
This non-compete clause applies within India and any jurisdictions where the Disclosing 
Party actively conducts business.

3.3 Exception
The non-compete restriction does not apply to general skills and knowledge retained in an 
individual's unaided memory.

SECTION 4: INDEMNIFICATION

4.1 Indemnification Obligation
Each party (as the Receiving Party) shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other 
party (as the Disclosing Party) from and against all claims, damages, losses, costs, and 
expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or resulting from:
- Breach of this Agreement
- Unauthorized disclosure of Confidential Information
- Misuse of Confidential Information
- Violation of intellectual property rights

4.2 Notice of Claims
The indemnified party shall provide prompt written notice to the indemnifying party of any 
claims subject to indemnification.

4.3 Defense
The indemnifying party shall have the right to control the defense of any claim, provided 
it keeps the indemnified party informed of proceedings.

SECTION 5: GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

5.1 Jurisdiction
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India, 
specifically the laws of the State of Karnataka, without regard to its conflict of law principles.

5.2 Exclusive Forum
Both parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in 
Bangalore, Karnataka. Each party waives any objection to venue in such courts.

5.3 Dispute Resolution
Before initiating legal proceedings, the parties agree to attempt resolution through good 
faith negotiations for thirty (30) days. If unresolved, the matter may be referred to 
arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

5.4 Governing Authority
The interpretation and enforcement of this Agreement shall be subject to the authority and 
jurisdiction of the Karnataka High Court.

SECTION 6: EXCEPTIONS TO CONFIDENTIALITY

Confidential Information shall not include information that:
a) Is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement
b) Is rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without breach of 
   any confidentiality obligation
c) Is independently developed without reference to the Confidential Information
d) Is required to be disclosed by law or court order, provided the Receiving Party gives 
   prompt notice to allow the Disclosing Party to seek protective measures

SECTION 7: TERM AND TERMINATION

7.1 Term
This Agreement shall commence on the date first written above and continue for a period of 
THREE (3) YEARS, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Section 2.

7.2 Renewal
Either party may request renewal by written notice at least 90 days before expiration.

SECTION 8: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Neither party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or 
punitive damages arising from this Agreement, even if advised of the possibility of such 
damages.

SECTION 9: ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject 
matter and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings. Any modifications must be 
made in writing and signed by both parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.

DISCLOSING PARTY:

Company Name: _________________________
Authorized Signature: _________________________
Name and Title: _________________________
Date: _________________________

RECEIVING PARTY:

Company Name: _________________________
Authorized Signature: _________________________
Name and Title: _________________________

"""
